About

I'm a gender and diversity specialist and ADHD coach

I provide ADHD coaching for adults, both face to face and online, specialising in but not limited to late diagnosed women navigating hormonal fluctuations and perimenopause and menopause. I help people harness their ADHD in order to achieve their goals and lead a fulfilling life, one that plays to their strengths and abilities and suits their rhythms.

Working as a gender, diversity and inclusion specialist for over 24 years, I bring an intersectional, women's empowerment lens to this work.

Using a strengths-based, neuroaffirming approach, and building on my own lived experience, and continued struggles, with ADHD, I am especially committed to helping empower and build confidence, giving clients the tools to flourish. I provide evidence-based support, guidance to relevant resources, and most importantly, a non-judgmental ear.
